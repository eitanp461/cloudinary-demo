Issue 04 — Field Film

Film by Yaroslav Shuraev

A morning, indoors.

Rain all week, so the film we came home with isn't the coastline at all. It is a borrowed kitchen, a hat that never came off, and a host who kept refilling the glass until the weather made up its mind.

Words & edit — the Wander desk

Field notes

We had planned four days on the water and got one. The rest went the way those weeks go: a table by the window, a radio nobody turned off, and the slow business of learning a household's timings — when the kettle goes on, which chair is not yours, how long a shower counts as short.

Sixteen seconds is all we kept. It is the closest thing to a portrait we have of the trip, and it was shot standing still, indoors, waiting.
