### Selection criteria

Images were chosen to avoid material that could complicate redistribution in a demo:

- no prominently depicted, recognizable people
- no visible brand names, logos, or signage
- no artwork, murals, or other separately copyrightable works in frame
- landscape, city, ocean, mountain, and nature subjects only

The `place` names shown in the site captions are editorial styling for the demo. The
authoritative locations, where the photographer recorded one, are: Tunnel View, Yosemite
(hero), Pragser Wildsee, Italy (mountain lake), and the west coast of Ireland (road).

## Video

One video, from the free Pexels library under the
[Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). `video-before/assets/host-at-home.mp4`
is the unmodified 4K original as downloaded from Pexels, renamed only. `/video-after/`
serves that same original through Cloudinary, resized and re-encoded for delivery. No
frame is re-timed or replaced, and on every landscape viewport the full 1.896:1 frame is
intact. Portrait phones are the one exception: there the film is cropped to 4:5 around the
subject, so roughly 42% of each frame's width is kept. That is a framing decision, not a
correction — the original composition is what the wider viewports show.
